## What is WaaP?

**WaaP (Wallet as a Protocol)** is a crypto wallet that's simple to use and secure by design. There's no seed phrase to write down or lose — your keys are protected using advanced security that runs behind the scenes.

### What makes WaaP different

- **No seed phrase** — Sign in with your email, phone, Google, or Apple
- **Your funds stay yours** — No one, including WaaP, can access your funds without you
- **Works everywhere** — Use WaaP across any dApp on Ethereum, Optimism, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Sui, Stellar, and more
- **Built for agents too** — AI agents can use WaaP wallets via the CLI with scoped permissions and policy controls
- **Built-in transaction scanning** — Every transaction is checked for scams and suspicious activity before you sign
- **Multiple recovery options** — Lost access to a login method? Recover through any other method you've set up

### Trusted security

WaaP has been independently audited by [Cure53](https://cure53.de/), [Hexens](https://hexens.io/), [Least Authority](https://leastauthority.com/), and [Halborn](https://www.halborn.com/), with additional security consulting from Anderson Software and [Distrust](https://www.distrust.co/). Learn more about how it works on our [Security](/security) page.

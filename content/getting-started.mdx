# Getting Started

Set up your WaaP wallet in minutes.

## Create Your Wallet

1. Visit the app where WaaP is integrated
2. Click **Connect** or **Sign In**
3. Choose your login method:
   - Email
   - Phone
   - Google
   - Apple
4. Complete verification
5. Done — your wallet is ready

## No Seed Phrase?

That's right. Instead of a secret you have to keep, your account holds an authorisation on a public chain that every signature has to be checked against, and your policy runs before any signature is produced.

This means:
- No seed phrase to lose, and nothing on your device worth phishing
- Your limits are enforced before a signature exists, not reported to you afterwards
- The authorisation is recorded on chain, not in our database

If you want a second, independent party involved in every signature, that is [WaaP Squid Mode](/squid-mode).
